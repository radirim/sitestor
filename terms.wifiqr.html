2. What the App Does

The App scans QR codes and barcodes with your camera or from images in your gallery. For WiFi QR codes, it reads the network details embedded in the code, shows them (including the password if the code contains one), and lets you connect or copy the password. It also decodes other formats such as contacts, URLs, text, locations and product barcodes, keeps a history of your scans on your device, and can generate QR codes, for example to share your own WiFi with guests.

Not a hacking tool. The App only reads legitimately shared QR codes. It does not crack, bypass or hack WiFi networks, and it only decodes information that is already contained in the QR code you scan.

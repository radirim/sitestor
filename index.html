About RMR Solutions

A Studio Built Around the Android Ecosystem

RMR Solutions is an independent Android development studio. We focus exclusively on the Android platform — which means every decision, every tool, and every line of code is optimized for Google's ecosystem.

We believe great apps are simple, fast, and transparent. We avoid unnecessary permissions, unnecessary complexity, and unnecessary data collection. Users trust the apps we build because we earn it.

  • Kotlin-first development with Jetpack Compose support
  • Published apps on Google Play with verified reviews
  • Full compliance with Google Play policies and GDPR
  • Transparent privacy practices — no hidden data sharing

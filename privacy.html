Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected and processed by RMR Solutions ("us", "we", or "our") when you use any mobile application published by RMR Solutions in the Google Play store.

This Privacy Policy is in full compliance with the GDPR in EU and COPPA in the US.

1. Collection of Information

We do not collect any Personal Information that specifically identifies you personally (such as your full name, physical address, email address, or precise geolocation).

The only Personal Information collected by our apps is a unique device identifier for advertising (e.g., Google Advertiser ID).

2. Processing and Usage

We use the information to provide personalized advertisements to non-child users (with consent) and to analyze general statistics to improve our apps.

3. Sharing of Information

We disclose information only to Google AdMob to serve advertisements. If you are in the EEA, we share your advertising ID only if you have given explicit consent.

4. Children's Privacy

In apps targeted to children, we do not collect or share any Personal Information. For general audience apps, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 16 without parental consent.

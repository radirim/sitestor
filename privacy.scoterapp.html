Scooter Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy covers the Scooter Manager Android app (package name com.scootermanager.ride), published on Google Play by RMR Solutions ("we"). Contact: info@rmrsolutions.online.

Summary

The app has no accounts. Your rides stay on your phone and we do not receive them. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. If you buy the optional Premium subscription, Google Play handles the payment and RevenueCat checks that your subscription is active. The app downloads map images from OpenFreeMap.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your precise location while it is open to show you on the map, measure speed and record routes. It does not use your location when you leave the app or lock the phone. Rides are stored only on your phone, and automatic backup is turned off. If you export a ride, you choose where the file goes.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to find and connect to your scooter and read its data. It does not use Bluetooth to find your location. On Android 11 and lower, the system requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning. The Bluetooth log stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

Advertising

In the free version, Google AdMob shows ads in the app. Google may collect device identifiers, including the advertising ID, app activity, diagnostics and approximate location from your IP address, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and UK, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice under More, Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Premium subscribers don't see ads, and the app doesn't request ads for them.

Purchases and subscriptions

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under your Google account and its own privacy policy. We never receive your card or bank details, your name or your Google account email from the purchase.

To unlock Premium and check that the subscription is still active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service that acts on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID that the app creates, which is not linked to your name or email;
  • the Google Play purchase token and your purchase details: product, price, currency, purchase and renewal dates, trial status, cancellations and refunds;
  • basic device and app information, such as the app version, Android version, store country and language, and your IP address, which it uses to connect and to work out your country.

We use this information only to provide Premium, restore it on a new phone, answer support and refund requests, prevent fraud, and keep the financial records the law requires. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell it.

VIP support

If you contact support, including Premium VIP support, we receive your email address and what you write to us. We use it only to answer you and keep it for as long as needed to handle your request. To help with a subscription or refund, we may ask for your Google Play order number.

Maps

Map images are downloaded from OpenFreeMap, based on OpenStreetMap data. OpenFreeMap can see your IP address and the map area you view. Your saved routes are not sent.

Ratings

If you rate the app, your rating is handled by Google Play under your Google account.

How long data is kept

Rides and settings stay on your phone until you delete them. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and Google Play are kept for as long as your subscription is active and then for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Support emails are kept while needed to handle your request.

Deleting your data

Use More, Delete all my data, or uninstall the app, to remove rides and settings from your phone. Deleting data or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.

To ask us to delete your purchase data held by RevenueCat, or your support emails, email info@rmrsolutions.online with your Google Play order number. We may keep records that the law requires us to keep. You can delete a Google Play review from your Google account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Email us to make a request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
